AI Image Generators Still Can't Spell — Here's How I Fix Text Without Regenerating

PhotoTextEdit · Tutorial · By MyToolster

You generate the perfect hero image. Then you zoom in: "COFEEE SHOP". Regenerating is a lottery — the next roll might fix the text but ruin the composition.

There's a better way: edit the text in place.

The workflow

  1. Upload the image to PhotoTextEdit
  2. Mark the misspelled region
  3. Type the wrong word and the correct replacement
  4. Download — the tool inpaints the fix, matching font, lighting, and perspective

Where this actually matters

OG images for blog posts, documentation screenshots, demo mockups, cleaning up AI-generated marketing assets. Anywhere re-shooting or re-generating costs more than a two-minute fix.

Limitations

Tiny text, ornate scripts, and complex curved surfaces are still hard. And don't use it on documents where text integrity matters (IDs, certificates).

I used PhotoTextEdit for this — it's free and runs in the browser.
