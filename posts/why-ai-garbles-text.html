Why AI Image Generators Garble Text — and How to Fix Misspelled Words Without Regenerating

PhotoTextEdit · Deep Dive · By MyToolster

Diffusion models are miraculous at images and terrible at spelling. Here's why — and what to do about it.

Why the text comes out wrong

  • Letters as textures. Diffusion models predict pixels, not characters. A letter is just another texture pattern to them.
  • Encoder compression. Text encoders like CLIP/T5 compress prompts into embeddings that preserve meaning but lose exact character order.
  • Training data. The web is full of distorted, stylized, and low-res text in images — the model learns that "text" often looks blurry and wrong.

Why regenerating is a bad fix

Re-rolling changes everything — composition, lighting, style. You might get the spelling right on roll #12 and lose the image you actually wanted. Prompt engineering ("spell C-O-F-F-E-E correctly") helps marginally but doesn't fix the underlying architecture.

The in-place fix

Edit the text where it sits: upload the image, mark the wrong word, type the correction. Modern inpainting fills the region while matching the surrounding font, lighting, texture, and perspective. Three practical tips:

  • Keep the replacement roughly the same character count as the original
  • Fix one word at a time on dense text
  • For 3D/neon/stylized text, use a dedicated mode if available
